📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

🛒 Overview
An end-to-end interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Amazon sales data — uncovering purchasing patterns across customer demographics, product categories, delivery performance, and payment behavior. Built to turn raw transactional data into actionable business insights at a glance.

✨ Key Features & Functionalities
📌 KPI Snapshot — Instant view of total purchase amount (₹19M) right at the top for quick decision-making
🌐 Online vs Offline Sales — Donut chart comparing purchase channels to understand customer buying behavior
👥 Demographic Insights — Gender-wise and generation-wise (Gen-Z vs Others) breakdown of customers
📈 Sales Trend by Store — Bar chart showing purchase amount distribution across different online marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho & more)
🗺️ Geo-Level Performance — Treemap visualizing order volume across major Indian cities (Pune, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, etc.)
💳 Payment Method Analysis — Pie chart breaking down UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Cash on Delivery, Net Banking & Wallet usage
📅 Year-over-Year Comparison — 2023 vs 2024 sales performance tracking
🚚 Delivery Speed Analysis — Treemap comparing Standard vs Express vs Same-Day delivery preferences
🎯 Dynamic Filters — Slice and dice data by Category, Brand, Festival/Sale events, and Location for deep-dive analysis
🔍 Cross-filtering & Drill-through — Fully interactive visuals that respond to selections in real time

⚙️ Tech Stack
🔹 Power BI Desktop — Core BI tool for data modeling, DAX calculations & visualization
🔹 Power Query — Data transformation & cleaning (ETL)
🔹 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) — Custom measures like Sum of Purchase Amount
🔹 Data Modeling — Relationship management across Category, Brand, Location, Payment & Delivery tables
🔹 Excel/CSV — Source data ingestion

🎯 Business Value
This dashboard empowers stakeholders to identify top-performing sales channels, optimize delivery strategy, understand festival-driven sales spikes, and tailor marketing by customer demographics — all through one clean, interactive interface.
